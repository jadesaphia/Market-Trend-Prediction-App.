Markets Mad Scientists

Markets Mad Scientists is a market-trend analysis application designed for students, beginners, researchers, small business owners, and individuals interested in understanding market information.

The goal of the project is to collect market data, display historical patterns, identify market trends, and generate understandable predictions.

Future development will include a real market-data API, secure user accounts, database storage, watchlists, and a Python machine-learning backend.
